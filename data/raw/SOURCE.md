# Source data

`nhtsa_recalls.csv` and `nhtsa_complaints.csv` are real, public NHTSA data for GM vehicles
(model years 2021 to 2025), downloaded from the NHTSA Recalls and Complaints APIs on
2026-11-04 by [catalog-steward-kit](https://github.com/AlexaGonzalesTuesta/catalog-steward-kit).

The dealer, vehicle owner and repair order tables are generated by `legacy/build_legacy.py`.
Every person in them is fictional.
